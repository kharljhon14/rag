## Prinny Photostrips: What They Are

Prinny Photostrips are photobooth-style photo strips printed from your own photos by Prinny Prints & Crafts. Each strip has 3 or 4 photos stacked vertically, with a decorated header or footer where we can add a name, date or short message. They're popular as keepsakes, gifts for couples and friends, and souvenirs for birthdays, weddings and debuts. Every photostrip is printed, cut and packed by hand.

## Prinny Photostrips: Sizes and Paper

- Photostrip size: 2 x 6 in (classic photobooth size)
- Photostrip layouts: 3 photos or 4 photos per strip
- Photostrip double set: two 2 x 6 in strips printed side by side on one 4 x 6 in sheet, cut apart or left uncut
- Photostrip paper: 260gsm glossy or matte photo paper
- Photostrip frame designs: Classic (white or black border), Floral, Minimal, or Custom (your own colors, theme or event design)

## Prinny Photostrips: Photo Guidelines

- Photostrip photos: clear, well-lit photos print best. Dark or blurry photos will look darker and blurrier on paper.
- Photostrip photo size: at least 1200 x 900 px per photo
- Photostrip photos are cropped to fit each frame. Tell us if anything near the edges must stay in the photo.
- Screenshots and photos saved from Facebook or Messenger are often compressed and may print blurry
- Black-and-white or sepia photostrips are available at no extra cost. Just let us know when you order.

## Prinny Photostrips: Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I order just one photostrip?**
Yes. Photostrips can be ordered as a single strip, starting at ₱25.

**Do you bring a photobooth to events?**
No. Prinny Prints & Crafts doesn't offer an on-site photobooth service. We print photostrips from photos you send us, before or after your event.

**Can I use different photos for each strip in a bulk order?**
Yes. Each strip can have its own photos. Send the photos in folders or albums labeled per strip so we can match them correctly.

**Can I use my own frame design?**
Yes. Send it as a 2 x 6 in design at 300 DPI (600 x 1800 px), with the photo areas left blank or marked. We'll check that it prints well before we start.

**Can I add our names and wedding date?**
Yes. Text on the header or footer is included in the price for every photostrip.

**Can I order photostrips as wedding or birthday souvenirs?**
Yes. Bulk prices start at 30 strips. For 100 or more strips, order at least 7 business days before your event.
