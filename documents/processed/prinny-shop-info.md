## File Guidelines for Customer Designs

- Preferred formats: PDF (best), PNG or JPG at high quality
- Resolution: 300 DPI at actual print size. Images taken from Facebook or Messenger are often compressed and may print blurry.
- Bleed: add 3 mm (0.125 in) on each side for designs that go edge-to-edge
- Safe margin: keep text and important elements at least 5 mm (0.2 in) inside the cut line
- Color: CMYK is preferred. Bright RGB colors (neon greens and electric blues, for example) may print slightly duller.
- Fonts: outline or embed fonts in PDFs, or send the font files
- Canva users: download as "PDF Print" with "Crop marks and bleed" turned on
- Send files through Messenger as documents, not as photos, so they aren't compressed
